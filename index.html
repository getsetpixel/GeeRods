Repairs

Get your rod back fishing fast.

Snapped tips, worn guides, cracked blanks and tired finishes — we fix them all. Fast turnaround, premium materials, and a result that looks and performs like new.

Repair services

What we fix

✦

Tip replacement

Snapped or damaged tip sections replaced with matched blanks and re-wrapped guides. Most jobs completed same day.

✦

Guide replacement

Worn, cracked or missing guides replaced with quality SIC or titanium frames, re-wrapped and epoxy-sealed.

✦

Blank repair

Stress cracks and minor fractures stabilised and reinforced — we'll assess and advise honestly on whether repair is worthwhile.

✦

Handle refurbishment

Cork and EVA handles cleaned, filled and reshaped. Reel seats tightened or replaced. Looks and feels brand new.

✦

Full refinish

Complete strip-back, re-wrap and epoxy coat — ideal for cherished rods that deserve a second life.

✦

Reel seat replacement

Loose, cracked or corroded reel seats removed and replaced with quality fittings, properly bedded and sealed.

Turnaround

Fast, honest, no fuss

Most standard repairs are turned around within 48 hours of receiving your rod. We'll always give you an honest assessment — if a repair isn't worth doing, we'll tell you.

  • Free assessment on all repairs
  • No fix, no fee guarantee, but we would be happy to discuss alternative solutions
  • Tracked return shipping available
  • Updates at every stage
