Every rod that leaves the GeeRods workshop starts the same way: with a conversation.

Not a product configurator. Not a dropdown menu. A proper conversation about where you fish, what you target, how you cast, and what you've always felt was missing from the rods you've used before.

## Why bespoke?

There's a reason professional anglers don't fish off-the-shelf rods. A blank chosen specifically for your casting style and target species performs differently — more sensitively, more efficiently — than the closest production equivalent.

But custom rods aren't just for professionals. They're for anyone who wants a tool that genuinely fits the way they fish.

## What goes into a GeeRods build

Every build includes:

- **Blank selection** — we source from trusted UK and European suppliers and hand-pick the right blank for your spec
- **Guide positioning** — placed by eye and by feel, not copied from a factory plan
- **Thread wrapping** — done by hand, in your choice of colour, finished with a hard-wearing epoxy coat
- **Handle fitting** — cork, EVA or split-grip, shaped to your hand if needed
